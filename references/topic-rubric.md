# Topic scoring rubric

Use this rubric to compare candidates. Score each dimension, calculate the total, then apply any risk penalty.

| Dimension | Weight | High-score standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Recency | 20 | The event is recent, still developing, or newly relevant now. |
| Audience relevance | 25 | The target reader is directly affected or strongly interested. |
| Novel angle | 15 | The article adds a distinct audience-specific question, test, or interpretation. |
| Practical value | 15 | The reader can make a decision, try a method, or avoid a mistake. |
| Depth potential | 15 | Reliable evidence supports a substantive article rather than a short recap. |
| Evidence quality | 10 | Claims are supported by primary or otherwise authoritative current sources. |

## Risk penalty

Subtract points after scoring:

- Minus 5: narrow shelf life or unclear local availability.
- Minus 10: material facts are disputed, incomplete, or hard to verify.
- Minus 15: legal, reputational, medical, financial, privacy, or safety risk needs specialist review.
- Reject: the premise depends on rumor, deception, harassment, plagiarism, or unsupported allegations.

## Interpretation

- 85–100: publish first while the window is open.
- 70–84: strong candidate; improve the angle or evidence.
- 55–69: keep as a reserve or combine with another topic.
- Below 55: discard unless the user has a strategic reason to cover it.

Do not let a high recency score compensate for weak audience relevance or weak evidence.

## Category checks

### Hot topics

- Name the current event and its date.
- Explain why the target reader should care.
- Add analysis or action beyond summarizing the news.

### Tools

- Verify the tool or feature from an official source.
- Identify a concrete task, user, and usage scenario.
- Note important pricing, access, privacy, or regional limitations.

### Cognitive viewpoints

- Express the central claim in one sentence.
- Identify the mechanism, evidence, and strongest counterargument.
- End with a useful implication instead of an empty attitude.

## Shelf-life labels

- Immediate: best within 24–72 hours.
- Short: useful for about 1–2 weeks.
- Medium: useful for about 1–3 months.
- Evergreen: durable, with a current hook that can later be refreshed.
